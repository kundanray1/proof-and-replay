# Code of conduct

Proof & Replay welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, focus criticism on the work, accept good-faith feedback, protect private information, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that undermine a safe, productive project.

Report conduct concerns privately to **Kundan Ray** at [raykundan57@gmail.com](mailto:raykundan57@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
